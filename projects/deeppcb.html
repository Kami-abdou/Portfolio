01Intro

DeepPCB is InstaDeep's AI-powered, cloud-native PCB routing software. It routes complex boards without requiring the user to be a routing specialist, which is the pitch the site had to land.

That framing set the design problem. The audience spans hardware engineers who know exactly how hard routing is and decision-makers who mainly need to understand the claim, so the site had to be credible to the first group without being opaque to the second.

02The pages

Ten pages at desktop width: the home page, an enterprise page and pricing, an editorial pair of blog index and article template, and the supporting help, contact, terms and 404 pages.

Designing the whole set rather than only the home page is what keeps a marketing site coherent — the legal and error pages are where consistency usually breaks down.
