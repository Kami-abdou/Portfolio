# UnDrive Website

Tags: UI design, UX design

## 👋🏼 Intro

---

As a freelancer at Inspire, a development agency, we worked on a project for a driving school. This platform allows users to search for driving schools in any location and filter details to find what best suits their needs.

Our collaborative effort resulted in the creation of an intuitive, user-friendly platform. The website's features, such as the location-based search and detailed filters, offer a personalized experience for each user. Now, finding a driving school that fits one's specific requirements is just a few clicks away. This project was a significant step towards enhancing the convenience and efficiency of choosing a driving school.
