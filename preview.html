About me

01

I am a senior Product Designer with a decade of experience building scalable digital experiences. Currently, I work full-time at InstaDeep, where I specialize in Product Design and robust Design Systems tailored for complex, next-generation AI products.

My journey has taken me from hands-on design for fast-growing startups across fintech, e-learning, and healthtech, to serving as Chief Product Officer (CPO) at Steer, where I led product strategy and advanced tech trends in the automotive space.

Today, I operate seamlessly at the intersection of UX strategy, advanced interface design, and emerging technology. My focus is turning highly technical, AI-driven capabilities into intuitive, high-performing user experiences that bridge the gap between complex backend data and elegant frontend design.

Outside the design sandbox, you can find me optimizing my routine at the gym, diving into video games, or recharging with family and friends.
