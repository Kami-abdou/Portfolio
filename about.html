About

I started in UX/UI design in 2020. Since then I have worked with eight companies across AI, fintech, food delivery, automotive retail, pharmacy and home services.

From October 2021 to November 2023 I was Chief Product Officer at Steer, leading a small team of front-end and back-end engineers while being the only person in design and product. The company went on to raise in France and is now based in Versailles.

I am now a senior product designer at InstaDeep, working on AI products — among them DeepPCB, their cloud-native PCB routing software, and the design system the company's product surfaces run on.

Outside of work I am obsessed with video games and enjoy the gym, and I cherish time with family and friends.
