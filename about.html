About

I entered the UX/UI design field on 1 January 2020. Since then I have worked with eight startups across fintech, food delivery, online courses, automotive, pharmacy and development.

I joined Steer as Chief Product Officer, where I led a small team exploring new technology trends in the automotive industry and designing and developing a range of automotive services. In practice the role was product design and product management.

I am now a full-time product designer at InstaDeep, working on DeepPCB — their AI-powered, cloud-native PCB routing software.

Outside of work I am obsessed with video games and enjoy the gym, and I cherish time with family and friends.
