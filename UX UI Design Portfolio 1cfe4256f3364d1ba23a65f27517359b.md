# **Hello! I’m Abdallah Yaackoubi.**

 I love working in the realm between UX and UI design. Things that make me excited are User centered design, Interaction design, and making interfaces feel fun and human.
Currently, I'm a Full-time Free-lancer Designing interfaces for STEER , HYPHEN GROUP and INSPIRE

---

- **About me**
    
    I entered the UX/UI design field on 2020 January 1st . Throughout my career, I have worked with 8 startups in various industries such as fintech, food delivery, online courses, automotive, pharmacy, and development. Two years ago, I joined Steer as a Chief Product Officer. In this role, I led a small team in exploring new technology trends in the automotive industry and designing and developing a variety of automotive-related services.
    
    Currently, I am fully focused on designing interfaces as a freelancer. I have been supporting companies and projects in this capacity for some time now.
    
    I have a passion for working at the intersection of UX and UI design. Some areas that particularly excite me include user-centered design, interaction design, and creating interfaces that are engaging and user-friendly.
    
    Outside of work, I am obsessed with video games and enjoy going to the gym. I also cherish spending time with my family and friends.
